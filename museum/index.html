Museum Donations

The Mistria museum is the catch-all for completions: fossils, lost relics, ancient texts and now (post-0.13.x) written artifacts in the library wing. Donation rewards roll out at fixed completion milestones.

Museum quickstart

The museum opens after the spring 8 main-quest trigger. Once unlocked, donations work the same as in similar farming RPGs — slot the artifact into the donation counter. Reward tiers unlock at specific completion percentages.

Fossil wing (40 artifacts)

Fossils come from digging artifact spots in the upper mountain biome, the museum's donation path also unlocks a fossil display in your home when you complete the wing. Each fossil type has at most 4 variants (skull, body, tail, fin).

Relic wing (35 artifacts)

Relics come from a mix of monster drops, fishing chests, mine artifact nodes, and the "Forge a Bridge" side quest. The relic wing also unlocks the Cartography dialogue chain with Balor if you complete the wing before year-two summer.

Library wing (20 artifacts, new in 0.13.x)

Patch 0.13.x added the library wing — written artifacts (scrolls, maps, journals) are now accepted, alongside the original fossils and relics. Library artifacts mostly come from:

  • Reading a specific book for the first time (recipe book counts)
  • Trading with the caravan on festival days
  • Donating a duplicate recipe book from the inn
  • Completing the Juniper 6-heart anniversary dinner cutscene

The library wing rewards a "Cartographer" title when complete and unlocks a unique museum map room for your home.

How to find artifact spots

  1. Walk the world with a hoe equipped.
  2. Look for the small crack-pattern ground tile (an X or worm-shaped indicator).
  3. Hoe to dig. Each spot yields one of: clay, coal, artifact.
  4. Artifact spots respawn every 2 in-game days.

FAQ

Are donated artifacts recoverable?

No. Once donated, an artifact cannot be retrieved. Don't donate rare quest items unless you've confirmed they're not used in a different quest chain.

What if I'm missing one artifact?

Check the Krobus-style traveling merchant; artifact tokens can be traded at the museum gift shop for the missing slot.

Heads up: Library wing artifacts are timestamped in 0.13.x. Don't accidentally trade recipe books to NPCs before donating them.
