Best fish for early-game cash

  • Catfish in spring (rainy days) — high base price, easy to find.
  • Dorado in summer (sunny days) — strong price + easy cast on the river.
  • Midnight Carp in fall — high price, low cost, no bait needed.

FAQ

Are some fish only available after a certain patch?

A small handful of late-game fish (Lava Eel, Glacierfish) were added in mid-EA patches. Check the patch notes for additions.

Where can I get bait?

Vida at the fishmonger sells bait daily; some is also a Loved-tier gift from Wesley.

Heads up: Fish prices and locations are pulled from the community wiki and the official fish guide. Patch updates occasionally rebalance; cross-check before launching a fishing-only farm.
