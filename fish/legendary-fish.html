Legendary Fish

Each season has at least one Legendary fish (the community wiki calls them the four "Seasonfish"). These are high-value, weather-dependent and require specific bait. Use this as a season-by-season checklist.

All Legendary fish

Each Legendary awards a trophy and significantly boosts your fishing skill. The catch window is short — about 6 in-game hours — so plan around it.

Spring Seasonfish (Mountain Lake)

  • Location: Mountain Lake, on the upper cliff above the plaza.
  • Season / Weather: Spring, sunny only.
  • Time window: 6 am – 12 pm game time.
  • Bait: Starflower Bait (from the inn kitchen).
  • Tackle: Cork Bobber (optional, makes the catch easier).
  • Reward: 1500g base, +30 fishing XP, trophy wall slot.

Summer Seasonfish (Ocean)

  • Location: South dock, ocean tile.
  • Season / Weather: Summer, sunny only.
  • Time window: 6 am – 2 pm.
  • Bait: Lava Eel Bait (crafted or bought from the traveling caravan).
  • Tackle: Quality Bobber.
  • Reward: 2000g base, +40 fishing XP, the "Tideborn" achievement.

Fall Seasonfish (Forest Pond)

  • Location: Forest pond, west of the abandoned smithy.
  • Season / Weather: Fall, sunny only.
  • Time window: 6 am – 11 am.
  • Bait: Honey Glaze Bait (unlock via Juniper's first cooking scene).
  • Tackle: Cork Bobber.
  • Reward: 1800g base, +35 fishing XP, "Woodland" achievement.

Winter Seasonfish (Forest Pond, ice biome)

  • Location: Forest pond, with the winter pond-melt cinematic.
  • Season / Weather: Winter, sunny only.
  • Time window: 8 am – 1 pm.
  • Bait: Glacier Bait (from the ice biome of the mine).
  • Tackle: Quality Bobber.
  • Reward: 2200g base, +50 fishing XP, "Snowmelt" achievement.

General strategy for any Legendary

  1. Pre-craft the bait the day before, out of regular harvest — don't waste the catch window hunting for bait mid-fight.
  2. Bring a Cork + Quality Bobber if you have both. Two tackle rods let you swap on long fights.
  3. Position yourself ON the correct tile (cast indicator will glow golden when you're on the right one).
  4. Most Legendaries require 5 successful minigame rounds. Each round is 8 to 14 seconds.
  5. Save before the cast. If the minigame fails, reload — RNG can rob you of the catch on a bad click.

FAQ

Can I miss a Legendary and still catch it later?

Yes. Each Legendary respawns every year. You can miss it in spring of year one and still catch the same fish in spring of year two.

Are there non-seasonal Legendaries?

A small handful (Lava Eel, Glacierfish) act like semi-Legendary fish outside the four-season structure. They are tracked on the museum donation checklist.

Heads up: Bait recipes for Legendary fish sometimes unlock via romance cutscenes (Juniper's first cooking scene, Caldarus's 4-heart). Plan your romance timing to align with which Legendary you want first.
