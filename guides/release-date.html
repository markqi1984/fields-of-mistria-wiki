Steam release: yes, on Early Access

The official Steam store page lists Fields of Mistria as a single-player Early Access title, with new content drops roughly every 6 to 8 weeks. The current build (0.13.x line at time of writing) is fully playable from start to year-end and includes marriage, farming, fishing, mining, cooking and museum donation loops.

Early Access means:

  • Save files can be safely carried between patches, but very old saves may need a one-time wizard run after a major version bump.
  • Some NPC schedules briefly break on patch day; an offline patch usually rolls within 48 hours.
  • Bug reports submitted through the Steam launcher reach the dev team directly.

Nintendo Switch release

A Switch edition is on the developer's public roadmap and was first mentioned in the developer's community update roughly a year after Early Access began. As of the current build, there is no firm release date. Players searching "Fields of Mistria Switch" are usually looking for one of three things:

  • Is it confirmed? Yes — announced as part of the multi-platform roadmap.
  • Will my save transfer? Cross-save is not confirmed. The Steam build currently uses a cloud save inside Steam's own ecosystem.
  • Will it cost extra? Not announced. The Early Access does not currently include a Switch key.

What still isn't in the game

The public roadmap focuses on three remaining pillars. Each appears under the "Coming Soon" tag on Steam and on the developer blog.

  • Multiplayer co-op farms — listed as "in design" on the roadmap, not yet implemented.
  • Additional marriage candidates — Caldarus, March and Juniper are confirmed; the 12-slots roster is stable across patches.
  • Localization for additional languages — community translations exist as mods; official localization scope is not yet public.

Should I start playing in Early Access?

If you like Stardew Valley, Coral Island or Roots of Pacha, yes — the current build already contains enough systems to comfortably clear 80+ in-game hours without hitting a content wall. The two rough edges to be aware of are the occasional save migration on patch day and the multiplayer feature still being unimplemented.

If you specifically want the Switch physical version, wait for an official release-date announcement before buying anything on Switch storefronts that lists a date — there are a lot of fake "release confirmed" posts floating around.

FAQ

Is Fields of Mistria fully released?

No. It is in Steam Early Access, with the full launch on the developer's roadmap but not yet dated.

Can I get it on Switch right now?

Not officially. The Switch version is announced but not yet on the eShop.

What happens to my save if I buy now?

Your save is preserved across patches. On major version bumps, a short migration wizard runs the first time you load the game.
