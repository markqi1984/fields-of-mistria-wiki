Fishing in 6 steps

From walking up to a river tile to having a fish in your pack: about 30 seconds of real-time once you know the rhythm.

1. Find water

Every map has at least one fishing tile. The river south of the farm, the mountain lake above the plaza, and the ocean at the south dock are the three locations new players hit first. Pick any of them.

2. Equip your rod

Press the fishing-rod slot (default Q) or click the rod icon to equip it. The training rod is what you start with — it lets you catch most beginner fish without a level-up.

3. Cast at a fish-shadow tile

Stand so your cursor hovers a water tile with a fish shadow. The shadow shape tells you what kind of fish is there: small shadows are common fish, large shadows are usually rare or Legendary. Click to cast.

4. Watch the bobber

The bobber lands in the water and waits. There is a small bobble motion before the actual bite — don't click too early. The bite is when the bobber fully submerges and "ducks".

5. The minigame

When the bobber "ducks", a vertical bar appears showing the fish's escape distance and your catch progress. Click and hold to reel in, release when the bar fills. Each fish has a different catch difficulty:

  • Beginner fish = 1 round, very forgiving bar
  • Intermediate fish = 3 rounds, bar fills faster, escape accelerates
  • Rare fish = 5+ rounds, brief spikes in escape speed
  • Legendary fish = 5 rounds, multiple tackle slots recommended

6. The fish goes to your pack

Successful catch: fish appears in your inventory, and a small XP tick goes toward Fishing skill. Skill levels unlock tackle recipes and bait at certain thresholds.

Bait basics

Bait is optional but worth picking up. It increases bite rate by about 25% and lets you catch fish you wouldn't otherwise meet. Three beginner bait types:

  • Standard Bait — purchased from the fishmonger, increases bite rate.
  • Wild Bait — crafted, increases both bite rate and the chance of double-fish.
  • Magic Bait — late-game, lets you catch any-season fish in any season (within reason).

Tackle basics

Tackle attaches to your fishing rod for extra effect. Two beginner tackles cover 90% of your first year:

  • Cork Bobber — makes the catch bar easier to manage.
  • Quality Bobber — increases silver+ gold fish chances.

First 5 fish to catch (any player, any spring)

  1. Smallmouth Bass — river, base catch.
  2. Bream — river, sunny days.
  3. Catfish — river or lake, rainy days, much higher price.
  4. Sardine — ocean tile, easy to farm for cooking recipes.
  5. Sunfish — river, sunny days, decent XP per catch.

Energy management

Each cast drains about 3 energy. Avoid fishing on the day before a festival if your energy is already low — failing a Legendary minigame mid-day is more painful with no stamina. A cooked meal refills ~75 energy, so plan around mealtimes.

FAQ

Can I fish while it rains?

Yes. Some fish (Catfish, Walleye, Eel) are weather-gated and only bite in the rain.

Does bait run out?

Yes — most bait is a single-use consumable per cast. Magic Bait is reusable.

Heads up: The catch-timing window in patch 0.13.x was widened for the training rod. If you missed the timing on older patches, give it another shot — late-game patches are more forgiving.
