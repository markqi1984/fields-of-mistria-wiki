Beginner Tips & First Week Guide

Starting a new life in Mistria can feel overwhelming. This guide walks you through the most important actions for your first seven days, common mistakes to avoid, and the fastest ways to build early-game income and friendships.

Day 1-3: Setup & First Crops

  • Clear your farm – Remove weeds, rocks, and logs. Each gives resources and raises your foraging skill.
  • Buy Parsnip seeds – They grow in 4 days and sell for a small profit. Plant at least 15.
  • Meet every villager – Introducing yourself to all 30+ villagers unlocks their schedules and gift preferences.
  • Check the community board – Early quests reward gold, seeds, and friendship points.

Day 4-7: Expand & Explore

  • Upgrade your watering can – Visit the blacksmith with 5 Copper Bars. A copper can waters 3 tiles at once, saving massive stamina.
  • Unlock the mine – Complete Mayor's bridge repair quest (opens Day 5). Mining provides ore for tool upgrades and gems for quick cash.
  • Try fishing – Buy a rod from the beach shop. Fish sell well and restore stamina when cooked.
  • Start gifting – Give loved items to villagers on their birthdays for 8x friendship boost.

Best Early Crops by Season

Spring Parsnip, Potato, Cauliflower

Parsnip is fastest. Potato has a chance for extra yield. Cauliflower takes 12 days but sells for high profit per tile.

Summer Blueberry, Melon, Tomato

Blueberry regrows after first harvest = best ROI. Melon is a single big payout. Tomato regrows and is needed for cooking.

Fall Pumpkin, Cranberry, Grape

Pumpkin sells for the highest single-crop price. Cranberry regrows and makes excellent jelly. Grape is needed for wine later.

Winter Winter Root, Crystal Fruit

Fields are frozen. Forage in the forest and mine for resources. Winter is the best time to upgrade tools and clear farm debris.

Stamina Management

Stamina is your most limited resource early on. Priority order:

  1. Water crops first (they die if skipped).
  2. Use upgraded tools to reduce stamina per action.
  3. Eat food before stamina hits zero (passing out costs 10% of your gold).
  4. Go to bed before 2 AM (late sleep reduces next-day energy).

Money-Making Tips

  • Pick up everything – Foraged items sell for small amounts but add up. Some are also loved gifts.
  • Process crops – Turn vegetables into pickles and fruit into jelly with preserves jars. Processed goods sell for 2x base price.
  • Fish on rainy days – Rare fish spawn more frequently. Check the weather forecast on your TV.
  • Mine on lucky days – The fortune teller on TV predicts luck. High luck = more gems and ladder drops.

Common Beginner Mistakes

  • Planting too many crops without upgraded watering can – You will run out of stamina.
  • Ignoring friendships – Villager hearts unlock recipes, cutscenes, and farm help.
  • Selling all ore – Save at least 10 bars of each type for tool upgrades.
  • Forgetting the museum – Donating artifacts rewards seeds, crafting recipes, and rare decorations.

Next Steps After Week 1

By the end of your first week you should have: a small crop field, a copper watering can, basic friendships with 5+ villagers, and access to the mine. Week 2 focus: build a chicken coop, expand to 30+ crops, and start saving for house upgrades.
