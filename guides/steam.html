Where to find the official page

Go to store.steampowered.com/app/2143790/. Avoid reseller sites that paste the same screenshot set; the only seller-of-record is Valve. From the page you can:

  • Wishlist the title so it surfaces in seasonal sales.
  • Install the demo from the same store page, when one is featured.
  • Read the official dev logs linked from the right-hand "Updates" panel.

Price and regional pricing

Fields of Mistria sits in the standard indie farming-RPG band. At time of writing it has run a launch discount and a handful of seasonal sales. Regional pricing is available in most countries Steam supports, including the local currency option in supported APAC and LATAM regions.

Two things to watch for:

  • Refunds: Early Access titles are refundable for any reason within 14 days / 2 hours of playtime, the same as any other Steam title.
  • Gift copies: Steam gift a copy is supported; the recipient needs to be in a region that receives the same regional pricing tier.

Demo availability

A demo is offered on the store page when a developer wants to spotlight a new build (most recently for the 0.11.x patch). The demo is a tutorial-and-spring slice — enough to test the art style and the first-day player experience without spoiling later romance scenes.

To install: open the Steam client, search "Fields of Mistria", look for the demo entry, and click Play Free. Save files from the demo do not currently carry over to the full game.

Steam Deck verdict

Fields of Mistria is classified as Steam Deck Verified. Expect 50 to 60 FPS at the default 1280×800 / Medium preset, fan stays quiet on the LCD model, and the controls map cleanly to the Deck face buttons (no touchscreen required for the fishing minigame).

Mods on Steam Workshop

The Steam Workshop is enabled. The community posts replacement textures, additional NPC dialogue sets, and farm layout overlays. If you are new to mods, the safest order of operations is:

  1. Back up your save folder (Steam → Manage → Browse Local Files → copy the Saves folder).
  2. Subscribe to one mod at a time.
  3. Re-launch the game after each subscription change so the new files register.

FAQ

Can I gift Fields of Mistria to a friend?

Yes, through Steam's gift flow. Region must match.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

No native Mac/Linux build at time of writing; Proton support on Linux is partial — confirm in your distro's ProtonDB entry before relying on it.

Do I need to launch the game via Steam to play?

Yes. Steam DRM is required; the game launches only with a valid Steam client connection.
