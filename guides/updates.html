Latest patches and roadmap

The roadmap moves roughly every 6 to 8 weeks. Items below are tagged so you can scan to "what I care about" instead of reading every dev log.

0.13.x line (current build)

The 0.13.x line added the museum library wing, finalized Caldarus's 4-heart / 6-heart dialogues, and overhauled the animal breeding GUI so gestation timers are visible from the menu.

Added Changed Fixed

Added

  • New heart event scenes for March and Caldarus at 4-heart and 6-heart thresholds.
  • Museum library wing accepts written artifacts (scrolls, maps, journals) in addition to fossils.
  • Spring festival mini-quest rebalanced for solo play.

Changed

  • Marriage candidate sync routines run every 10 minutes instead of hourly, smoothing the heart-event pace.
  • Fishing cast energy cost reduced in spring, so the river is friendlier to first-week players.

Fixed

  • Corn seeds no longer consume the full packet on partial harvest failure.
  • Animal breeding GUI correctly shows gestation for pregnant animals.
  • A rare soft-lock when fast-forwarding the night's weather scene was resolved.

How to read patch notes efficiently

The Steam launcher keeps a separate patch log per build. If a change affects your playthrough (item ID, NPC behaviour, save migration), the log marks it with a small icon. The notes below are condensed for skimming — when you need exact wording, fall back to the Steam window.

FAQ

Do I lose my save on a major patch?

No. Patch day usually clears the cache but the save persists. On major version bumps a one-time migration wizard runs the first time you load the game.

Where do I report a new bug?

Steam launcher → right-click the title → Support → "Report a problem". The dev team triages these weekly.

Will I need to re-buy the game for Switch?

Not announced. Cross-save and cross-buy are typically clarified close to release.
