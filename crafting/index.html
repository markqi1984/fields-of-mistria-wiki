Crafting Recipes & Guide

Fields of Mistria features a robust crafting system that lets you turn raw materials into tools, furniture, decorations, and cooking ingredients. This guide covers every crafting station, essential early recipes, and tips to farm materials efficiently.

How Crafting Works

Crafting in Fields of Mistria requires two things: a crafting station and the right materials. Most recipes are unlocked automatically as you level up skills or discover new items. Some special recipes come from befriending villagers or completing quests.

Essential Crafting Stations

Station Workbench

The basic crafting station available from day one. Used for simple tools, fences, and basic furniture. Place it inside your house or farm shed.

Station Kitchen Counter

Required for all cooking recipes. Upgrading your house unlocks the kitchen. Early recipes include bread, salad, and simple soups that restore stamina.

Station Anvil (Blacksmith)

Used to craft and upgrade metal tools. Requires ore bars (Copper, Iron, Silver). Tool upgrades increase efficiency and unlock new gathering abilities.

Station Loom & Tailor Table

Turns wool and plant fibers into cloth, clothing, and decorative tapestries. Cloth is also used for some gift items villagers love.

Early-Game Must-Craft Items

  • Basic Hoe – Till soil for crops. Upgrade to Copper Hoe for 3x3 tilling.
  • Watering Can – Keep crops hydrated. Upgraded versions water multiple tiles at once.
  • Axe & Pickaxe – Chop trees and break rocks. Higher tiers clear stumps and large boulders.
  • Scarecrow – Protects a 5x5 crop area from crows. Essential for large farms.
  • Chest – Store excess materials. Craft multiple and label them by category.

Cooking Recipes Worth Learning First

Cooked food restores stamina and can be gifted. Priority recipes:

  1. Simple Salad – Any vegetable + dressing. Cheap and reliable.
  2. Fried Egg – Chicken egg + oil. Fast stamina recovery.
  3. Roasted Fish – Any fish + salt. Better than raw fish, good for mining days.
  4. Pancakes – Flour + egg + milk. High stamina restore, loved by several villagers.

Material Farming Tips

  • Place a recycling bin near your farm to turn trash into crafting components.
  • Check the traveling merchant every Friday and Sunday for rare crafting materials.
  • Upgrade your backpack early so you can carry more ore and wood per mining trip.
  • Save refined quartz for mid-game sprinklers and lightning rods.

Furniture & Decor Crafting

Once your house is upgraded, craft furniture to raise your home rating. Higher ratings unlock spouse events and extra storage rooms. Popular craftable items include bookshelves, potted plants, rugs, and seasonal decorations.
