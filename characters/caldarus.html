Caldarus profile

Caldarus first shows up at the abandoned smithy on the cliff south of town. He's been part of the village's lore since the founding story — but he's also a full romance candidate.

How to start Caldarus's arc

Caldarus is gated by the main quest: he won't open his 4-heart arc until you complete the "Forge a Bridge" milestone, which itself requires a level-2 town hall upgrade. Most players hit this naturally around mid-summer of year one.

  1. Reach town hall level 2.
  2. Receive the "Forge a Bridge" side quest from Juniper.
  3. Donate 10 ingots to the smithy reconstruction — this unlocks Caldarus's dialogue chain.

Schedule

  • Tuesday / Thursday / Saturday — at the smithy, accepts ingot donations.
  • Friday evenings — at the inn, performs a small music set you can attend for a +1 friendship.
  • Festival days — at the festival grounds, mild additional dialogue if your heart value is 4+.

Heart events

  • 2 hearts — Caldarus teaches you a smelting mini-game that unlocks bars worth +30% sell price.
  • 4 hearts — A flashback scene covering the dragon's history with the village. Required for the main quest to advance.
  • 6 hearts — Dual scene at the smithy: romance choice and story choice both presented. Either advances the main quest.
  • 8 hearts — A dragon-flight scene at dusk. Important for the post-credits setup.
  • 10 hearts — Proposal cinematic + main-quest resolution trigger.

Strategy: romance and the main quest in parallel

Caldarus is the rare candidate where romance IS the main quest. The two progress lines are the same line. So:

  • Treat every dragon-relic drop as a priority gift — they double as main-quest items.
  • On off-days (Monday / Wednesday / Sunday) when Caldarus isn't reachable, smelt bars to bank some as backup gifts.
  • Don't skip the 4-heart event — it gates the rest of his arc.

Marriage compatibility

Marrying Caldarus unlocks a unique post-game scene in the cliff smithy and routes more dragon-relics into your shipping bin. His spouse room is a forge space — be ready for a small fire ambience interaction during the first few in-game days.

FAQ

Do I need the main quest to romance him?

Technically no — you can gift him at any heart value, and heart events before the 4-heart scene still trigger. But to reach marriage, the "Forge a Bridge" milestone must complete.

Where do dragon scales come from?

Most reliably from the lava biome of the mine, level 80 and below. See the Updates page for the latest drop-rate patch notes.

Heads up: Caldarus's storyline is the most patch-sensitive candidate. The 0.11.x build added his 4-heart and 6-heart scenes; the 0.13.x line added a library wing tie-in.
