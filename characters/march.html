March · Character Profile

March is one of the twelve marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. She's the resident florist of the village — easy to find, easy to romance, and a frequent entry point for first-time players.

March profile

March is the first villager most new players meet because she's stationed near the central plaza. Her gift and heart-event chains are very straightforward, which makes her a good "tutorial romance".

Where to find her

March's regular schedule:

  • Mornings — by the fountain in the central plaza, watering her flower bed.
  • Afternoons — inside the museum, restocking the donation counter.
  • Festival days — wandering the festival grounds, easy to bump into with a +1 friendship on every dialogue choice.

Tip: March's dialogue changes the moment you give her a flower she has not seen in that save. Stack three unique flower gifts in her first week and she'll open up a side quest by heart 4.

Heart events

  • 2 hearts — March invites you to help weed her plot. Triggers a +1 friendship and a starter bouquet recipe.
  • 4 hearts — A short scene in the museum about a flower specimen she can't identify. Complete it for the "Botanist" achievement.
  • 6 hearts — She asks you to plant a special flower for her on the farm, which also unlocks her 6-heart clothing theme.
  • 8 hearts — Major arc involving the village's lost seed vault. Major choice: help her restore it or let it fade; both lines are still marriage-compatible.
  • 10 hearts — Bouquet event. After this, the proposal cinematic becomes available.

Best way to push March's arc fast

  1. Gift a Loved-tier flower on day 1 (Starflower from the spring seed packet is reliable).
  2. Speak to her every morning before leaving the plaza.
  3. Show up on her birthday (Spring 14) with the Diamond — that's a +250 friendship in a single day.
  4. Stack her 4 and 6-heart dialogues in the museum window so you don't miss the unique scenes.

Marriage compatibility

March is one of the most laid-back spouses. She stays on the farm during festivals, contributes flower-based artisan goods to the shipping bin, and her spouse room reads as a botanical workshop rather than a bedroom. Good pairing for first-time players who want to learn the marriage system without complex room re-decorating.

FAQ

Can I marry March in year one?

Yes — every romanceable candidate is marriage-eligible from the start. Push her to 10 hearts and gift a Mermaid's Pendant to propose.

What if I miss her birthday?

You can still gift her within 7 in-game days after for the doubled bonus. After that window, the friendship gain resets to standard tier values.

Heads up: Flower tiers and scene triggers are pulled from the community wiki. New seasonal flower varieties sometimes ship in patches and add to March's Loved list — check the patch notes page before finalizing your plan.
