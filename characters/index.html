Townfolk

Townfolk have no romance arc but are central to festivals and shop schedules.

  • Emery — runs the morning bakery counter before Reina takes over.
  • Lou — postal worker, delivers all gifts-and-mail items.
  • Tate — carpenter, upgrades your house and barn.
  • Vida — fishmonger, sells bait and the spring river map.

Tips for meeting every character in year one

  • Town visits first: most NPCs have a "first meeting" dialogue that boosts your starting heart count by 1 if you trigger it within the first 7 in-game days.
  • Festival days: every seasonal festival is a free +1 to every NPC you speak to. Use these as heart-farming milestones.
  • Mail a birthday gift: any villager's birthday doubles the friendship gain — schedule around it for the biggest heart jumps.
