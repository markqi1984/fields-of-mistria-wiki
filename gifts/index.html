Loved-tier rules

  • Universal loved items — Diamond, Iridium Bar, Mythril Bar, artisan wine of any base. Most NPCs accept these as Loved-tier. Use them when the per-NPC list above runs dry.
  • Birthday multiplier — Loved gifts on the candidate's birth season double the friendship gain. Schedule a Loved-tier gift on that week for the biggest jump.
  • Unstackable items — Diamonds count as +80 once per NPC per week. Stacking them yields diminishing returns.

Liked (+30) shortcuts

If you have artisan overflow at the end of the day, the easiest Liked-tier items across most NPCs:

  • Apple, Banana, Orange, Mango — most NPCs like at least one
  • Cooked meal of any quality — almost universally Liked
  • Most gemstones — Quartz, Topaz, Aquamarine
  • Vegetables — Cabbage, Cauliflower, Parsnip

What to avoid giving

Disliked (-20) items are real friendship loss. The safest rule: never give an item before checking the per-candidate Loved list. The most common wrong gifts to avoid:

  • Hot Sauce, Pepper, Spicy ingredients to most food-loving NPCs — many dislike these in lore
  • Raw fish to cooking-focused candidates like Juniper — they prefer cooked meals
  • Flowers to non-flora-focused candidates like Caldarus — explicitly disliked
  • Dairy to some miner / smithing-focused NPCs

Strategy: stockpile birthdays

  1. On day 1 of every season, check who's having a birthday that season.
  2. Pre-stack 3 Loved-tier gifts for the candidate so the morning-of is automatic.
  3. Gift immediately at 6 am on birthday morning for max friendship delta.
  4. Even on a bad day, a Loved-tier birthday gift usually adds +250.

FAQ

Do NPCs share Loved lists?

No, every candidate has their own tier. The items listed in our table are illustrative — the canonical list lives on the community wiki.

What if I run out of gifts?

Plain dialogue gives small heart ticks. Festivals double this. Save up for the next season's birthday if you have to skip a week.

Heads up: Loved lists shift between patches. Always cross-check the latest community wiki before committing a stockpile plan.
