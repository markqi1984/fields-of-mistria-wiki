Latest Patch Summary (Ver. 0.13.x line)

The current Early Access build continues to focus on marriage candidates, museum expansion and seasonal festival polish. Below is the rough changelog the official developer blog and the community wiki (fields-of-mistria.wiki.gg) track in parallel.

Added Changed Fixed
  • New heart event scenes for March and Caldarus (4-heart and 6-heart dialogues)
  • Museum gains a library wing — accepting written artifacts in addition to fossils
  • Spring festival mini-quest rebalanced for solo play
  • Fixed: Corn seeds no longer consume the full seed packet on partial harvest failure
  • Fixed: Animal breeding GUI correctly shows gestation for pregnant animals
Heads up: Fields of Mistria is in Steam Early Access. Patch content and feature timing change frequently — always cross-check the official patch notes on Steam before spending in-game currency.
