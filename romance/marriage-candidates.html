Marriage Candidates Ranked

There are twelve marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. They aren't all equally easy to romance. This page ranks them by gift economy, schedule consistency and main-quest tie-in so you can pick the candidate that lines up with your playstyle.

All 12 candidates at a glance

Tier list reflects ease-of-romance and main-quest fit for a brand-new save. Patch 0.13.x line assumed.

S-tier — easiest for first-time players

  • March — central location, cheap flower gifts, 4-heart arc unlocks a recipe book you need anyway. Read the profile: March.
  • Juniper — gates the main quest at heart 4, so you climb her arc naturally. Read: Juniper.

A-tier — low friction, slightly more setup

  • Ash — gift pool lines up perfectly if you focus on mining. Worth pushing if you cleared the lava biome before summer.
  • Balor — cartographer, loves maps. Less flashy but very chill schedule.
  • Reina — baker, sweet-treat recipient. Great if you spam cooking anyway.

B-tier — fun but require effort

  • Caldarus — main-quest-tied, but his gifts require late-game dragon relics. Read: Caldarus.
  • Elsie — librarian, gift pool needs museum donations.
  • Heldric — general store proprietor, gift pool is artisan crafts.
  • Nora — forager, gift pool is wood and natural goods.

C-tier — high effort, niche appeal

  • Minowa — dancer, only appears during the traveling caravan days (Spring 14, Summer 14, Fall 14).
  • Theron — adventurer / scholar, gift pool is relics and museum donations but his location moves around.
  • Wesley — beach-hand, only available on coastal maps (some are post-launch).

How the tiers were computed

  • Gift Economy (40%): how often the candidate's Loved pool overlaps with what a new player is already collecting for the main quest.
  • Schedule Consistency (30%): how predictable the candidate's location is across days and weather.
  • Story Tie-in (30%): how much of the candidate's arc unlocks content you'd want anyway (recipes, museum items, etc.).

FAQ

Can I rank these myself?

Yes — the tier list is a community aggregation, not a developer statement. If a candidate's heart-events line up with your playstyle, you'll naturally push them faster.

Which candidate gives the best spouse buffs?

Each spouse adds a small artisan-good drop. For new players, the cooking-buff carries best (Juniper's kitchen dialogue unlocks a +5% quality chance on cooked meals).

Heads up: All twelve candidates are fully implemented in the current build. Marriage unlocks are gated only by heart level — there is no soft lock requiring you to play late-game patches.
