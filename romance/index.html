Romance & Friendship System

Fields of Mistria runs romance on a 10-heart friendship meter, with each candidate unlocking side quests, gifts and unique dialogue as you climb. Here's the rule set, the weekly gift pattern that maximizes hearts, and the best way to switch targets if you change your mind.

How romance works

Single-player romance is opt-in for every candidate. There's no jealousy penalty if you switch — but weddings lock you out of every other candidate's proposal until you divorce or finish a full save wipe.

Heart thresholds and what they unlock

Heart valueUnlocks
0 → 2 heartsCandidate's name appears on the friendship HUD, recipe/book unlocks begin
2 heartsFirst cutscene, basic dialogue chain unlocked
4 heartsMost main-quest ties activate. Caldarus's arc, June's "Forge a Bridge" trigger, etc.
6 heartsMajor arc cutscene + unique clothing theme
8 heartsRomantic flag — bouquet gifting accepted, route locks here
10 heartsMermaid's Pendant proposal cinematic — marriage begins

Best weekly gifting pattern

A new player aiming for a clean heart-10 in 10 weeks should follow this routine:

  1. Mon / Wed / Fri — speak to every candidate once (small baseline friendship ticks).
  2. Tue / Thu — gift a Loved-tier item to the candidate you want to push to 10 hearts.
  3. Sat / Sun — fishing or mining day, but drop in to the inn for a small friendship ticking.
  4. Festival days — every seasonal festival gets you +1 friendship with every candidate you talk to, automatically.

Switching candidates mid-route

You can give to multiple candidates simultaneously. There's no penalty for not committing early. The only irreversible action is triggering the proposal cinematic (8 hearts + Mermaid's Pendant). After that, you can only switch through divorce (3000 gold in fees) or wiping the save.

Best starter candidates for first-time players

  • March — easy to find, loves flowers (cheap to source), tutorial-friendly dialogue chain.
  • Juniper — directly tied to the main quest, so you're pushed to heart 4 simply by playing normally.
  • Ash — if you focused on mining first, Ash's gift pool (metalwork + ingots) lines up with what you're already collecting.

Bouquet and proposal flow

  1. Reach 8 hearts with your chosen candidate.
  2. Buy a Bouquet from Pierre's flower shop (or the spring festival vendor).
  3. Gift the Bouquet. The candidate's dialogue flips to "romanceable" status (8+ hearts).
  4. Reach 10 hearts.
  5. Craft a Mermaid's Pendant (requires 5 Iridium Bars + 1 Pearl + 1 Coral — end-game materials).
  6. Gift the pendant between 6 am and 10 pm on the candidate's birth season to trigger the proposal cinematic.
  7. Wedding plays out 3 in-game days after the proposal.

What marriage changes

  • The spouse moves into a designated room on the farm.
  • Spouse contributes one artisan-good per day to the shipping bin.
  • Spouse dialogue overrides generic NPC dialogue during festivals.
  • Divorce is technically possible (3000 gold), but most fans treat it as a soft lock.

FAQ

Does gifting to multiple candidates cause jealousy?

No — Fields of Mistria has no jealousy model. You can gift everyone simultaneously until you propose to one.

Can I skip hearts faster with festivals?

Yes, festivals give +1 friendship to every NPC you speak to, so the more festivals you attend the faster you climb.

Heads up: Heart-event cutscenes trigger on the candidate's location, with weather and time-of-day gates. If a cutscene fails to fire, check the calendar — most require a specific day or weather.
