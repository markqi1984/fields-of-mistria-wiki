Mining Guide

The Mistrian mines are located north-east of town, past the old bridge. Mining yields ores, gems, geodes, and rare artifacts used for crafting, tool upgrades, and museum donations. This guide breaks down each mine level, enemy types, and the best strategies for profitable runs.

Mine Basics

The mine opens on Day 5 of Spring after completing the bridge repair quest given by Mayor. Each floor is procedurally generated with rocks, ore nodes, and enemies. A ladder appears once you break enough rocks or defeat all enemies on the floor.

Ore Types by Floor

Floors 1-20 Copper Ore

Copper nodes are brown-orange and common. Smelt into Copper Bars at the forge for basic tool upgrades and building materials.

Floors 21-40 Iron Ore

Iron nodes are dark grey. Iron tools are significantly more efficient. Also needed for sprinklers and certain crafting recipes.

Floors 41-60 Silver & Gold Ore

Silver appears from floor 41, Gold from floor 50. These are used for high-tier tools and valuable crafting items. Bring plenty of food for stamina.

Floors 61-80 Gems & Rare Ore

Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, and Diamond nodes spawn here. Gems sell for high prices and are loved gifts for many villagers. Diamond is the most valuable.

Floors 81+ Mythril & Ancient Artifacts

Mythril is needed for end-game tool upgrades. Ancient artifacts can be donated to the museum for rewards or sold to the archaeologist.

Enemies in the Mine

  • Slime – Basic enemy, drops slime gel (used in crafting). Weak to swords.
  • Bat – Flies toward you in swarms. Use a sword with wide swing arc.
  • Skeleton – Medium damage, drops bone fragments. Appears from floor 30+.
  • Ghost – Floats through walls, immune to normal attacks without upgraded sword.

Mining Day Strategy

  1. Bring food – At least 5 cooked items for stamina restoration.
  2. Upgrade pickaxe first – Higher-tier pickaxes break rocks faster and can hit multiple tiles.
  3. Clear floors efficiently – Focus on ore nodes and ladder rocks; ignore empty corners.
  4. Use elevator checkpoints – Every 5 floors you unlock an elevator shortcut, making return trips faster.
  5. Check the fortune teller – On lucky days, gem and ore spawn rates increase significantly.

What to Do With Geodes

Geodes are cracked open by the blacksmith for 25g each. They contain random minerals, artifacts, or rare crafting materials. Early-game, crack every geode you find. Mid-game, save money by only cracking geodes on high-luck days.

Mine Fishing Spots

Underground lakes appear on floors 20, 40, 60, and 80. These contain unique fish not found on the surface, including the Cave Eel and Lava Carp. Bring your fishing rod on mining days for extra profit.
