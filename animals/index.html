Animals & Breeding Guide

The animal system splits into Coop (chickens, ducks, rabbits) and Barn (cows, goats, sheep, pigs). This page covers the first animals to buy, the cheapest feed strategy, and the breeding GUI as it shipped in patch 0.13.x.

Coop animals

Coop animals live in the smaller building, eat cheaper feed, and reach maturity fast. Best starter picks for year one:

Barn animals

Barn animals are slower to mature but produce higher-value artisan goods. Recommended buying order:

  1. Cow — fast maturity, large milk yield.
  2. Goat — slightly faster than cow, lower milk volume but slightly higher value.
  3. Sheep — wool collection, valuable once turned into cloth.
  4. Pig — truffle forager, high value, requires outdoor access.

Feeding and happiness

  • Hay from the silo is the default. Build a silo early; it stores hay cut from grass or bought from Marnie's ranch.
  • Outdoor access makes animals happy and gives small bonus output. Don't lock them in all day.
  • Heater for winter — required for barn, optional for coop.

Breeding 0.13.x GUI

Patch 0.13.x added a visible gestation timer to the breeding GUI. To breed animals:

  1. Select two adult animals of the same species from the breeding menu.
  2. Confirm pairing. A gestation timer begins (3 to 5 in-game days).
  3. The pregnant animal shows a visible badge in the animals menu.
  4. After gestation, the offspring appears in the barn the next morning.

The 0.13.x fix: the badge now displays the remaining gestation days (pre-patch it just showed "pregnant").

Year-one recommended setup

  • Coop: 2 chickens, then add a duck on day 20 of spring.
  • Barn: 1 cow at day 30 of spring, then add a goat mid-summer.
  • Silo: built on day 1 to bank hay.
  • Feed strategy: buy hay when Marnie's stock is full, otherwise let animals graze outdoor grass.

Artisan loops

Most animal produce unlocks an artisan recipe at higher friendship levels:

  • Eggs → Mayonnaise machine
  • Milk → Cheese press
  • Wool → Loom (cloth)
  • Truffles → Truffle oil (highest value per input)

FAQ

Do animals die?

No. In the current build, animals do not die from neglect. Neglect lowers happiness, which lowers produce quality.

Should I buy pigs?

Only after your barn is fully upgraded and the silo can carry them. Truffles are valuable but pigs are expensive to feed.

Heads up: The breeding GUI changed in patch 0.13.x. If you last played in 0.11.x, the gestation timer is new — take advantage of it to plan your breeding schedule.
